# Validation guide

Every released pair is reviewed by a qualified speaker of the source language, who confirms
that the source sentence and its English translation correspond in both directions. Records
that fail validation are not included in a release.

Language-specific review guidance for Yorùbá diacritics and tone, Swahili regional variation,
and Nigerian Pidgin code-switching is maintained by the language working groups.
