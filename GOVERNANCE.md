# Governance

Structure from .

## Bodies
**Steering Committee** — sets direction, approves policy and roadmap, resolves disputes
the maintainers cannot. Meets quarterly, minutes published.

**Technical Maintainer Council** — owns the codebase, schema, release process and
reviewer qualification. Meets monthly.

**Language Working Groups** — one per language. Own that language's validation rubric
and validator qualification.

## Principles
This project holds African language data. Governance follows the
**CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance** — Collective Benefit, Authority to
Control, Responsibility, Ethics — alongside FAIR.

In practice: a majority of Steering Committee seats are held by people based in, or
institutionally rooted in, the regions whose languages are represented here. Language
Working Groups are chaired by native speakers.

## Becoming a maintainer
Contributor → reviewer → maintainer. Each step needs a sustained record of accepted
work and a nomination from an existing member at the next level. No appointment
by employment status alone.

## Decisions
Routine changes: maintainer review, two approvals to release.
Policy, licence, or scope changes: Steering Committee, decision recorded publicly.
